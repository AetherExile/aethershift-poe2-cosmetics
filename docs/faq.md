# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This FAQ addresses common technical, licensing, and operational questions regarding **AetherShift** for Path of Exile 2.

---

### 1. Does AetherShift grant permanent MTX ownership on my Path of Exile account?
**No.** AetherShift is a live client-side cosmetic customization tool. Supported visual selections do not require the selected MTX to be owned on your Path of Exile account. AetherShift does **not** grant MTX ownership, add cosmetics to your account profile, or modify account entitlement records.

---

### 2. Which platforms and launchers are supported?
AetherShift supports 64-bit **Windows 10** and **Windows 11** systems running Path of Exile 2 through either:
- The **Steam Client**
- The **Official Standalone Launcher** from Grinding Gear Games

Path of Exile 1, console editions, Kakao, and Tencent clients are not supported.

---

### 3. What cosmetic categories are available in AetherShift?
AetherShift supports character skins, character effects, back attachments, weapon effects, portal effects, cursor customization, and multi-tier MTX variants live in Path of Exile 2.

---

### 4. What is the 2-hour trial offer?
A 2-hour trial evaluation key is a planned product offer. Trial key availability and distribution schedules are announced operationally within the official [Aether Exile Discord Community](https://discord.gg/nxGqxpHSdK).

---

### 5. How are game updates handled when Path of Exile 2 patches?
When Grinding Gear Games releases a Path of Exile 2 patch, compatibility updates are issued to maintain software compatibility with the updated game client.

---

Official downloads, announcements, and support are centralized at:
- **Website**: [aetherexile.com](https://www.aetherexile.com/)
