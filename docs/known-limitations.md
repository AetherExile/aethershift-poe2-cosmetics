# Known Limitations

To maintain technical transparency, this document lists the operational scope, platform restrictions, and known limitations of **AetherShift**.

---

## 🔒 Scope & Technical Boundaries

### 1. Account Entitlements & Server Data
- **No Entitlement Modification**: AetherShift does not add MTX items to your Grinding Gear Games account profile or modify account entitlement records.
- **Client-Side Scope**: Visual customizations are applied locally on your client display.

### 2. Supported Games & Clients
- **Path of Exile 2 Dedicated**: AetherShift is developed for Path of Exile 2. Path of Exile 1 is not supported.
- **Windows Exclusive**: 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 are required. macOS and Linux are not supported.
- **Console Exclusions**: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S are not supported.

### 3. Display Settings
- **Custom Cursor Rendering**: Running Path of Exile 2 in Exclusive Fullscreen mode may prevent custom cursor display rendering. **Windowed** or **Borderless Windowed** mode is recommended when using cursor customization.

### 4. Game Updates
- Following major Path of Exile 2 patches released by Grinding Gear Games, a software compatibility update may be required before features resume operation.
